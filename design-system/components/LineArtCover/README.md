# LineArtCover

A 1200 x 720 SVG line-art cover, on the light ground by default or on a deep blue pair, for a project card, an article or a post, showing what the project does.

- **Make it** with `/card-illustration` (Claude draws it to the command's rules), or recolour existing art into the palette with its `recolor` style.
- **Ground:** a full-frame gradient, top left to bottom right: `light` (`page` to `page-wash`) by default; a deep blue pair only when a dark cover is asked for, `midnight` first, `navy`, `aqua` and `cobalt` for variety (the command's own names).
- **Lines:** 4.5 units (1.5px on screen), round caps and joins; connectors dashed at 60% opacity. On light, `primary` strokes and dots with white glass panels (white at 70%); on dark, white strokes with panels white at 10%.
- **One accent** for what carries the meaning, such as the result or the centre of a hub: on light, `butter` as a fill under a `primary` outline; on dark, aqua on navy and butter on the other pairs. Panel corners 22 to 32. No grid.
- **Check it** at its display size, with the subject inside the safe area the command describes.
