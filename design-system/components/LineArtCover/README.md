# LineArtCover

A 1200 x 720 SVG cover in white line art on a deep blue pair, for a project card, an article or a post, showing what the project does.

- **Make it** with `/card-illustration` (Claude draws it to the command's rules), or recolour existing art into the palette with its `recolor` style.
- **Ground:** one deep blue pair as a full-frame gradient, top left to bottom right: `midnight` first, `navy`, `aqua` and `cobalt` for variety (the command's own names for its pairs). Never a light pair.
- **Lines:** white strokes at 4.5 units (1.5px on screen), round caps and joins, no fill except panels (white at 10% with the white stroke); connectors dashed at 60% opacity.
- **One accent** for what carries the meaning, such as the active path, the result or the check mark: aqua on navy, butter on the other pairs. Panel corners 22 to 32. No grid.
- **Check it** at its display size, with the subject inside the safe area the command describes.
