How Company Brain keeps private things private

Three floors, one rule

Think of everything your company knows as one building. Your notes start on your own desk. They move upstairs only when you say yes, and nothing on a shared floor can point back down to them.

TOP FLOOR

Company

Everyone reads it. Changes are reviewed first.
Mission and valuesStrategyCompany goalsRoadmapOrg chartDecisions
Moves up with your yes, then a review Never down
MIDDLE FLOOR

Team

Everyone can read it. The team owns it.
Team goalsProjectsMeeting notesWeekly updatesTeam board
Moves up only with your yes Never down
YOUR DESK

Personal

Only you. Never shared or synced.
Daily notesWeekly notes1:1 notesPrivate goalsDraftsMy board

How your yes works

  1. 1
    You ask to share a note with your team or the company.
  2. 2
    Claude makes a clean copy. Anything under "Private" and any link to your desk is left out.
  3. 3
    You see exactly what will change before anything is written.
  4. 4
    Nothing moves until you say yes.
The one rule

Shared notes never link down to private ones. A built-in check flags it the moment it happens.
