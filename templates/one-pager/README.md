# One-pager templates

- `comparison.html`: an A4 page comparing two things side by side, on the light ground. A header band with the title, then two columns: a row-by-row table, what side A holds, what side B holds (grouped, with counts and a total), the key number, why both are needed and what they share. Copy it to `out/<slug>.html`, replace every `[BRACKETED]` slot and delete what you don't need; the comment at the top of the file has the fit and export rules. `/one-pager --type=comparison` starts from it.
