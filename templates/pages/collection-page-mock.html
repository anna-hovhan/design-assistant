Project 01

Lead router

Every inbound lead is scored, enriched and assigned to the right owner before anyone opens the inbox.

The problem

Leads arrived from three forms and a shared mailbox. Response times depended on who happened to be online, and high-value leads were missed.

The system

  1. Capture. Forms and email land in one queue.
  2. Score. A model rates fit and intent against past wins.
  3. Assign. The CRM owner is set and notified in Slack.
  4. Check. Borderline scores go to a human for one tap.

The outcome

Response time fell from 4 hours to 3 minutes, and nothing waits in the inbox overnight.

Project 02

Invoice agent

Supplier invoices are read, matched to purchase orders and posted, with exceptions sent to a person.

The problem

Finance retyped every invoice by hand. Mismatches were found at month end, when they were expensive to fix.

The system

  1. Read. PDFs are parsed into line items.
  2. Match. Each line is checked against its PO.
  3. Post. Clean invoices go straight to the ledger.
  4. Escalate. Mismatches open a review card.

The outcome

Manual entry time dropped by 82% and mismatches surface the day the invoice arrives.

Project 03

Support triage

Incoming tickets are classified, answered when the answer is known, and routed by urgency when it is not.

The problem

Agents spent the first hour of each day sorting. Urgent issues waited behind routine questions.

The system

  1. Classify. Topic, urgency and sentiment per ticket.
  2. Answer. Known issues get a drafted reply from the help centre.
  3. Route. Everything else goes to the right queue.

The outcome

Most routine tickets close on their own and urgent ones reach a person in minutes.

Project 04

Weekly reporting

The Monday performance summary writes itself and arrives in Slack before the first meeting.

The problem

Someone spent half a day each week copying numbers between tools, and the summary was often late.

The system

  1. Pull. Metrics are fetched from each source.
  2. Explain. A model writes what changed and why it matters.
  3. Post. The summary lands in Slack with charts.

The outcome

The team starts Monday with the numbers already explained.
