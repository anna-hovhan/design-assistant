# Glass layer stack

Concept art for "layers", "stack", "platform" or "architecture" stories. Reference: `reference.webp`.

**Look:** 3 to 5 frosted-glass slabs stacked like floors of a tower, widest at the bottom. Soft white studio backdrop with a faint floor reflection. Each slab carries small raised modules (cubes, a heat-sink grille, a cylinder), a few tinted periwinkle, blush or violet. Thin glowing blue rods pass vertically through the slabs. A scatter of loose glass blocks and chips sits on the floor, joined by faint light traces.

**Use:** hero image, cover or section opener. Label layers in your own layout; keep text off the art.

**Make it:** `node generate.mjs out.svg --layers=4` for the flat SVG version. For a rendered 3D look, use the prompt below with an image model and the reference.
