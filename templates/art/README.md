# Art templates

One folder per conceptual art piece, each with a `reference` image, a deterministic `generate.mjs` (plain Node, no dependencies) and a `README.md` that describes the look and gives an image-model prompt where useful.

- `glass-layer-stack/`: frosted glass slabs in a tower, for layers and architecture stories.
- `contour-wave/`: white contour lines on a navy-to-blush gradient, for flow and momentum covers.
